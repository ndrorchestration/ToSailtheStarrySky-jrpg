**November 3, 2026 | Unreal Engine 5.8.4 | Noncanonical review only; not native-render verified**

## Verified framing defect and intended correction

The saved V4 Skybourne three-quarter camera is at **(1050,840,560) cm**, aimed at **(0,0,40) cm**, 42° horizontal FOV. At 1280×720 the derived vertical FOV is approximately **24.37°**; downward pitch is **-21.14°**, so even the *highest* camera ray is **-8.96° below the local level horizon**. The entire old frame is down-looking and cannot show a natural sky-and-ocean horizon. **This is a composition limitation, not a verified root cause of the separate black-sky material failures.**

Independent existing UE 5.8.4 D3D11 local PNGs have confirmed four wing identities, updated cyan/gold wing leaves, and a prior black-majority percentage **83.33% / 83.68%**. Ship body, bridge sphere and habitation remain placeholders; these images were creator-unapproved. Review and actual verified hashes are in [#732 workstation inspection](https://github.com/ndrorchestration/ToSailtheStarrySky-jrpg/pull/732#issuecomment-6618883452).

**This isolated pilot** proposes another camera, **(900,1100,190) cm** looking at **(0,0,80) cm**, 42° horizontal FOV. Its approximate 720p upper ray is **+7.76°** above local level, yielding **~32% frame above the local horizon**. These are camera-geometry estimates, not an Unreal screenshot, a guaranteed unobstructed skyline, or proof of planetary curvature.

## Tool chain and safety

1. **Camera math:** unreal/Tools/plan_aureate_v4_horizon_review.py calculates the original and new view frusta using Python stdlib. Offline command: python unreal/Tools/plan_aureate_v4_horizon_review.py --output A_NEW_OUTSIDE_REPOSITORY_PATH.json. The --check flag verifies immutable generated bytes. It does NOT render.
2. **Native stage source:** unreal/Tools/stage_aureate_v4_horizon_atmosphere_review.py duplicates the original map and adds native review actors only in the new level. Uses **Full Editor** -ExecutePythonScript, never the fast -run=pythonscript commandlet for this actor creation task.
3. **Resource-gated host:** unreal/Tools/run_aureate_v4_horizon_stage.py --attempt horizon01 can launch a one-time *NullRHI* scene stage only after >=4.2 GiB free RAM, >=14 GiB disk, no running Unreal Editor/commandlet, four exact map digests, and unique unused evidence paths. Stops only its own process if the 180s watchdog or <0.95 GiB remaining RAM triggers. Staging is NOT a GPU render.
4. **Next native image:** once that stage has genuinely passed, derive a registered Editor.Python GPU capture pointing to the **NEW** scene and **NEW** camera. Existing #737 GPU tooling points to the old comparison camera and cannot stand in as new-shot proof. Require >=6.5 GiB free RAM and independent 1280×720 PNG image/hash, exposed pixels and visible atmosphere/sea check, then author review.
5. **Art/play QA:** compare old and new frames for hull/crew scale, actual authored glass geodesic bridge, ocean horizon, sun/sky atmosphere, wings and contrast at full and 50% thumbnail. Also require a playable-eye viewpoint and performance later.
