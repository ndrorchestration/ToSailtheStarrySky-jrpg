# First Wake — Revised Opening Storyboard (2026-10-27)

**Status:** CREATOR-DIRECTED PREMISE / DEVELOPMENT STORYBOARD. Not a playable implementation, approved full script, or promotion to main.
**Story authority:** newer explicit creator decisions supersede the September Lyra-first sequence and the older in-game opening; preserve the old work as history until native Godot adaptation is verified.
**Public-safe scope:** Wake Dock, Ender, Ionia, Lyra, Talen, Denith, ship failure and open fuel hypothesis. Do not publish restricted lunar/postgame material.

## Locked creator-directed story facts
1. Ender (22) urgently seeks paid work, finds **Captain Ionia's flyer** seeking a technician knowledgeable about harmonic levitation-based airship engines, and answers it.
2. Ionia (28), captain and owner, has spent months repairing the **Aureate Wake** after its engine fell out of phase and grounded her for roughly **one year**. She served her mentor Captain Denith for **ten years**; he died of old age and entrusted the ship to her.
3. **Ionia is the first recruit/active story companion** after Ender, before the initial test flight; recruitment order for the early story is **Ender → Ionia → Lyra**. Presence/plot resonance is not a grant of authorized combat actions.
4. Ender works **at the engineering station** during the controlled first flight. The brief flight ends in an urgent but managed **ocean splashdown**. The Wake is designed to remain **seaworthy**.
5. Diagnosis: recurrent **phase alignment** and **energy containment** problems; the unique engine also lacks the **specific fuel it requires**.
6. **Lyra**, Ionia's cousin and mother of **Talen (5)**, joins the story/party **after** splashdown. Give Lyra and Talen meaningful family introduction, everyday life and personal agency before the core anomaly investigation.
7. Ender, Ionia and Lyra co-develop an initial **fuel theory** using Ender's harmonic expertise, Ionia's operational knowledge and partial details Denith told her, and Lyra's sensory observations. Denith did not have time before his passing to teach Ionia all of the vessel's secret specifications.
8. Ionia's innate story energy is Earth, Ender's Spirit, Lyra's Water (with separate pink spirit-energy motif); these facts do not automatically grant spells, jobs or battle turns.
9. The protagonists' compact home ship **Aureate Wake** is not the separate larger **Dragonfly Frigate**.

## First-Wake narrative rhythm
Need → opportunity → provisional trust → competent small victory → systems failure → careful recovery → family contact → collaborative investigation → chosen commitment.

## Superseded chronology / preservation matrix
- **Former FW 00–04:** Wake Dock arrival, walk, berth work, Talen's "Are you staying?" before Lyra. **Adapt:** flyer and Ionia precede flight; berth/task can remain, but Talen's scene moves post-splashdown. Preserve interaction value and artwork; remap later.
- **Former Sequence 06:** Lyra joins immediately at "Wrong Song." **Superseded:** Lyra recruitment moves post-splashdown, once introduced through her relationship with Talen and Ionia; keep their distinct perceptions as a later clue.
- **Former Sequence 07:** Ionia acts only as non-playable practical guide. **Superseded:** Ionia is the **first** companion prior to test flight; split active party from combat-turn authorization.
- **Former Sequence 13/14:** Ionia joins on cast-off. **Superseded:** join before departure. Rework old quest flags and party assertions in native Godot; do not alter JS historical reference by default.
- **Former flight:** early open-sky flight and subsequent anomaly/hostile encounter. **Adapt:** controlled flight/splashdown must occur first; preserve later threats/choices as later chapter beats pending story review, not silently delete or force them into unsafe first flight.
- **The Wrong Song** and 2-of-3 clues: retain mystery and freely explorable investigation, but post-splashdown with Ender/Ionia/Lyra at the table/engine. Any original supernatural content remains subordinate to restricted story authority.
- **Denith:** mentor died of old age; incomplete teaching is a human unfinished conversation, not evidence of hidden malice.
- **Reson and Aepex:** later recruits; no invented early appearances, magic kits or lines in this opening.
