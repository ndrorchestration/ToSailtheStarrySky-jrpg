# First Wake — Opening Dialogue Pass v0.1 (2026-10-27)

**Status:** AUTHORED DIALOGUE CANDIDATES. These lines are **not** final creator-approved performances or implemented native scenes. Applicable to **Ender, Ionia, Lyra, Talen**, with Denith only quoted from Ionia's fallible recollection. Preserve existing historical screenplay instead of silently deleting it.

## Character voice locks
- **Ender (22):** earnest, technically precise, curious, slightly self-conscious; light humor when nervous. Knowledgeable about harmonics but not an instant miracle worker. He needs employment and a reason to feel useful.
- **Ionia (28):** seasoned navigator/captain despite youth; firm safety judgment, practical affection, quick dry wit; grief about mentor Denith without melodramatic conspiracy. Respects competence, admits missing knowledge.
- **Lyra (24):** emotionally observant and independent; a young mother with her own needs, not an exposition antenna. Frequency-sensitive; does not instantly resolve engineering.
- **Talen (5):** curious about ship details, drawings and blocks; age-appropriate questions and ordinary mischief. No prophecy, coded supernatural clues or battle agency.
- **Denith:** deceased (old age); only recollections and incomplete records. His exact undocumented words are invented *for review* and must not be treated as archival facts.

## FW-01 / First conversation at the Aureate Wake
- IONIA: "If you're here about the flyer, give me a moment. This panel only behaves when I don't watch it."
- ENDER: "I'm Ender. I understand harmonic systems. At least, the parts that behave on paper."
- IONIA: "Good. The Wake hasn't been behaving on paper or anywhere else."
- ENDER: "How long has she been grounded?"
- IONIA: "A year since the engines fell out of phase. Months of repairs. And one very patient dockmaster."
- ENDER: "Have you flown her since?"
- IONIA: "Not a full flight. I don't take people into the sky on optimism alone."
- ENDER (ask compensation): "Is this paid work?"
- IONIA: "Yes. Modestly, at first. I'd rather pay a careful engineer than another person who tells me she needs replacing."
- ENDER: "I'm very much in favor of not replacing the ship."
- IONIA: "Then you can start by telling me what's wrong without pretending you know what you don't."
*Provisional employment agreed; story-active Ionia joins Ender, not an invented battle turn.*

## FW-02 / Engineering first task
- IONIA: "That ring sets the phase reference. This one limits containment. Don't touch the third until you tell me what you think it does."
- ENDER: "You're testing me."
- IONIA: "I'm protecting my ship. The two aren't mutually exclusive."
- ENDER (inspect regulator): "The standing readings are close. The correction is almost too clean."
- IONIA: "Meaning?"
- ENDER: "We might have calibrated it for a ship that isn't moving."
- IONIA: "And under load?"
- ENDER: "I don't know yet. We should find out carefully."
- IONIA: "That's the first answer today I've trusted."

## FW-03 / Denith and the inheritance
*Optional: an old maintenance annotation beside the helm; this is not a complete hidden specification.*
- ENDER: "These notes are in two hands."
- IONIA: "Mine and Denith's. I worked with him for ten years. He taught me to keep her in the sky."
- ENDER: "And now you're captain?"
- IONIA: "He left her to me. He didn't have children. I suppose he thought ten years was enough time for me to learn everything."
- ENDER: "Was it?"
- IONIA: "For navigation? Maybe. For everything inside this ship? No."
- IONIA: "There were things he meant to show me after the next overhaul. He died before we got to them."
- ENDER: "I can help with what we can measure."
- IONIA: "Good. I won't ask you to invent the rest."
*Optional follow-up:*
- ENDER: "Were the engines always this unusual?"
- IONIA: "They were particular. Denith knew what they liked. I knew how to keep the crew and routes safe. I didn't realize how much was left between those two things."

## FW-04 / Preflight and first lift
- IONIA (helm): "Engineering, status?"
- ENDER (engine room): "Containment stable. Phase reference within our agreed margin. I still want the first circuit short."
- IONIA: "So do I. This is a test, not a celebration."
- ENDER: "Could it be a very small celebration?"
- IONIA: "Once we've landed."
- IONIA: "Moorings clear. Power rising. Aureate Wake, let's see what you remember."
*The ship lifts; hold the ocean vista long enough for player wonder.*
- ENDER (quietly): "Oh. She really flies."
- IONIA: "Of course she flies."
- IONIA (beat): "I was starting to forget how much I missed that."

## FW-05 / The drift and containment emergency
- ENDER: "Captain, the phase is drifting."
- IONIA: "How quickly?"
- ENDER: "Faster than the correction can follow. Containment is working harder to keep up."
- IONIA: "Reduce load. I'm taking us toward open water."
- ENDER: "The reserve dropped again. It shouldn't have fallen that far."
- IONIA: "Can you hold the engine steady?"
- ENDER: "For a short descent. Not for a return to dock. And I won't gamble with that."
- IONIA: "You won't have to. Brace for water."
*Player stabilizing action branches:*
- ENDER (good input): "I can give you a cleaner descent. Not more flight time."
- ENDER (incorrect input): "No. That setting feeds the drift. Try the containment bypass marked safe for descent."
- IONIA: "We are not saving the engine at the expense of the hull."

## FW-06 / Ocean splashdown
*Ship contacts water, settles and remains afloat. Short silence before reaction.*
- ENDER: "We're... floating."
- IONIA: "Yes. The Wake is seaworthy."
- ENDER: "You might have mentioned that earlier."
- IONIA: "You were busy keeping the engines from tearing themselves apart."
- ENDER: "Fair."
- IONIA: "Crew first. Check the containment. Then tell me what we actually have."
- ENDER: "One intact captain, one very relieved engineer, and a ship that would appreciate a break."
- IONIA: "An excellent starting inventory."

## FW-07 / Diagnosis, no easy fix
- ENDER: "Two recurring problems. Phase alignment and containment under load."
- IONIA: "And the fuel?"
- ENDER: "The usable reserve is gone. I think the engine burned through it trying to correct itself."
- IONIA: "It's not ordinary fuel. Denith was very particular about where he took it aboard."
- ENDER: "Do you know what was in it?"
- IONIA: "No. I know where the reserve used to sit, how it was handled, and that substitutions were never acceptable."
- ENDER: "Then buying something that looks similar would be a terrible plan."
- IONIA: "We've reached our first agreement since the splashdown."

## FW-09 / Wrong Song moved to the shared investigation
*Start with Lyra helping with ordinary shipboard tasks; a subtle pulse or missed note draws her attention.*
- LYRA: "Wait. Something changes when you turn that regulator."
- ENDER: "The readings say it shouldn't."
- LYRA: "I didn't say the readings were wrong. I said it changes."
- ENDER: "Right. Sorry. What do you notice?"
- LYRA: "Not a louder note. A note that doesn't settle."
- IONIA: "Denith used to listen for that."
- ENDER: "Did he explain why?"
- IONIA: "Not in a way I understood then."
- LYRA: "We can start with what each of us knows, instead of guessing what he meant."
*Clue nodes in any order:*
- ENDER / phase chart: "The reference shifts only when the engine is doing useful work."
- IONIA / Denith log: "The transfer schedule was strict, even when the tanks weren't empty."
- LYRA / resonance observation: "It feels less like the note disappears and more like nothing holds it in place."

## FW-10 / Three minds, first fuel theory
- ENDER: "I think we've treated the phase drift, the containment strain, and the missing fuel as separate failures."
- IONIA: "Aren't they?"
- ENDER: "Maybe. But the timing matches too closely."
- LYRA: "If the fuel has something to do with the sound holding together..."
- ENDER: "It might not only feed the engine. It might help it stay aligned."
- IONIA: "Denith told me once that the Wake wouldn't fly on power alone. I thought he meant the crew."
- LYRA: "He may have meant both."
- ENDER: "That's a theory. Not a formula. We still need evidence."
- IONIA: "And we need a safe source of fuel, whatever it turns out to be."
- LYRA: "Then we investigate together. I want to know why this ship matters to our future, not just why it stopped flying."
- IONIA: "Agreed. No more test flights until we know what we're putting into her."
*Theory board labels: OBSERVED phase drift; OBSERVED containment stress; OBSERVED depleted specialized reserve; REPORTED Denith guidance; HYPOTHESIS stabilization role; UNKNOWN composition/source/origin.*
