# First Wake — creator-corrected Hilltonia → offshore splashdown route

**Decision origin:** Creator's explicit in-conversation correction, November 16, 2026.  
**Authority:** Narrative and scene-placement requirement for the Unreal 5.8.4 vertical slice; **NOT** evidence of installed/integrated gameplay.  
**Implementation state:** Existing opening V5 harbor E×7 route is historical **LEGACY REVIEW**, not a creator-approved playable narrative. This document supersedes that route's intended scene placement, without deleting its prior test evidence. **DRAFT, no merges, no production asset approvals.**

## A. Player sequence — location, action, and state dependencies

| Order | Location | Player / ship action | Mandatory authorization or visual cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Deciduous forest at Hilltonia's inland edge | Ender **starts in the forest**; follows an accessible woodland path into the town, can explore | New Game owns forest spawn; no flyer auto-granted; harbor remains seaward to the east |
| A2 | Hilltonia town square | Encounters a **small, charming ROUND stone water fountain** with decorative moving/standing water, beside a community posting board | Both are locatable and legible; fountain is scenery **not ocean physics**; posting board is the real flyer discovery target |
| A3 | Ionia's flyer **on the board** | **Read** flyer to learn Ionia needs a harmonic engineer | Reading alone does **not** accept the commission, recruit Ionia, or expose later quest objectives |
| A4 | Same board | Explicit **Accept quest / Not now** player prompt | Only acceptance enters the active quest. Declining leaves the flyer available. A new waypoint/quest marker identifies Captain Ionia **only after acceptance** |
| A5 | Hilltonia harbor → Aureate Wake dock | Ender follows marker and meets **Ionia standing beside the docked ship** | Dedicated short conversation: invitation to assess the **ship's MAIN ENGINEERING ROOM** and secure preflight checks. Ionia does not appear at the helm yet |
| A6 | Ship gangway / entrance | Player **targets the Aureate Wake itself** (separate from Ionia) to board | Gate on accepted quest + completed Ionia conversation; shipboarding is not completed by talking to Ionia |
| A7 | Aureate Wake **main engineering room** (interior) | Ender investigates harmonic levitation engine; checks Lift, Pressure, Reserve and phase baseline; secures final preflight and engineering signoff | All interactive instrumentation must be **physically inside the ship**, not on the dock or exterior. | A9 | Ship underway | **Ionia commands harmonic levitation activation** from the bridge | Skyborne transformation begins **after** clamps release, eastbound heading, underway movement and explicit captain order; demonstrate four dragonfly wings moving into flight configuration and rising out of water |

Ender diagnoses and tries controlled adjustments, but the Aureate Wake mysteriously fails to hold settings | Cause not explained/solved prematurely; communicate ship's personality through puzzling gauges/controls, not an arbitrary scripted magic failure |
| A12 | Rapid descent → ocean **far offshore** | Ionia and Ender coordinate glide, damping and emergency contact | Player/cinematic demonstrates efforts; **physical water contact and sustained stability**, no teleported milestone, unrelated beach or coincidental legacy Denith cache |
| A13 | Offshore ocean, immediate aftermath | Ship returns to **Seabourne**; hull afloat, crew assesses the result | No catastrophic damage: only stressed components / degraded levitation systems. Ship is now **plot-locked to Seabourne for several story acts**; Skyborne/Submersible/Starbourne remain unavailable |
| A14 | Onboard recovery discussion | Ender and Ionia regroup, diagnose and debate what to do next | Lyra is NOT in the preflight or first flight party; she is introduced only after actual splashdown/initial regroup |
| A15 | Post-splashdown encounter | Lyra contributes her own knowledge and intuitive feel for the Aureate Wake, then **explicitly joins the party** | She is the first new recruit after Ionia; no automatic recruitment merely on water contact. Her son Talen remains part of established canon, but scene logistics must be separately authored, not invented as a shortcut |
 
**Story rhythm:** The ship is a character and a mystery: Ender is an experienced harmonic-resonance engineer, yet the Aureate Wake's anomalous core resists familiar fixes. Ionia values him for real skill, but the system is not an ordinary levitation plant. Their partnership begins through work and a shared emergency, not a premature technical resolution.

## B. Scene ownership and production authority

- **Forest/Hilltonia exterior:** world-scale, east/west town road; woodland connects to town; dock extends **east into water**. Forest-start PlayerStart and robust navigation are new required work. **Town-square posting board** must be authoritative flyer trigger. The currently added `HilltoniaPostingFlyerPreview` and round fountain are **review-only visual geometry**, not a working quest or approved art.
- **Harbor and ship exterior:** Ionia at ship; quest marker after explicit acceptance. Interact Ionia dialogue, then target the ship independently to board. Old seven `TSTSS_PRELUDE_REVIEW_*` actors along pier must not function as player-facing finished route.
- **Ship interior:** `/Game/TSTSS/Review/VerticalSlice/VS02_AureateInteriors` is a review starting point, but it must be upgraded to a credible full main engineering room connected to real ship interior and bridge comms. Lift/Pressure/Reserve/phase check and final signoff **inside**. The optical `M_EnginePresentationReview` is optional lookdev, not itself evidence of integrated engine-room progression.
- **Takeoff/flight:** Eastward heading and movement, retracting clamps, Ionia order, Skyborne wing deployment/lift, acceleration, altitude, phase drift, Ender compensation, worsening descent, cooperative glide. Cinematic camera and audio must preserve scale and input authority; sound/music route pending rights/audio QA.
- **Physical water/recovery:** Existing `VS04_SplashdownSea` physics-cube adapter proved *proxy* immersion/flotation in a prior bounded native review. Replace with authored Aureate Wake hull/sea boundary for actual playable ship-contact acceptance; don't silently promote physics proxy. The existing `VS05_DenithFuelShanty` legacy level is **not** a creator-approved immediate post-splashdown mystery/cache. No geographic coincidence, early recovery or Lyra auto-grant.
- **Ship modes:** Four-wing Skyborne flight; immediately after emergency landing the ship takes four raised vertical, slightly aft-raked, independently rotatable solarpunk wind-sail wings in Seabourne (separate [draft #846](https://github.com/ndrorchestration/ToSailtheStarrySky-jrpg/pull/846) is a visual hinge review, not a complete transformation). Starbourne stays **fail-closed**.

## C. Ordered implementation queue and gates

1. **P0 narrative/state topology:** add a distinct read vs quest-accept state, Ionia conversation gate, ship-target boarding gate; revise objective/HUD/compass markers. Ensure restart/save/versioning explicitly cover the acceptance state. Reject skips, wrong map, or duplicate grants.
2. **P0 geographic authoring:** new isolated forest-start Hilltonia route with real PlayerStart, town center, fountain, authentic board interaction and reliable all-way road/dock traversal. The existing V5 seven-marker pier route remains historical QA but is **not** final.
3. **P0 engineering-room placement:** ship interior map, genuine entry from gangway, full interior stations, intercom and Ionia-at-helm staging. Prevent exterior technical interactions and preflight signoff anywhere else.
4. **P0 departure/flight:** clamp → east heading → underway → captain command → Skyborne transformation → genuine ascent and high-altitude, phase instability → Ender compensation failure → glide.
5. **P0 offshore sea and crew:** actual Aureate hull contact and buoyancy, immediate Seabourne with 4 wind sails, partial system stress, recovery dialogue, Lyra joining **after** water and conversation.
6. **P0 acceptance:** independently run two complete native player-driven title→forest→town square→board→Ionia→ship interior→launch→sea→Lyra flows, physical check, resume/restart, save continuity and negative-path checks, source hashes and screenshots. Creator visually reviews before approval; instrumented OS keys do not replace that signoff.
7. **P1 art and experience:** detailed timber/stone town and fountain, legible board, proper Ionia/Ender/Lyra rig/material, ship engine-room geodesic/helm lighting, wing cinematics, subtle responsive dashboard/intercom, weather, storm and audio.

**Negative authoring requirements:** No automatic flyer acceptance; no Ionia marker before acceptance; no inspection stations at the waterfront; no premature launch/flight; no automatic mystery cure; no emergency landing near a convenient planted Denith component; no Lyra before splashdown/crew regroup; no catastrophic ship destruction; no Starbourne access or premature full four-form campaign.
