## Authority and privacy discipline

1. Pages contain historical entries; **do not treat all statements on a page as currently simultaneously true**. Most recent *explicit creator decision* supersedes older conflicting art/title instructions, but newer timestamps alone do not settle canon.
2. These links are provenance, not a promise that every agent has a Notion connector or that Notion pages are public.
3. Do not export source artworks, Notion attachments, hidden character/ending revelations, or private records to the public repository without a separate rights/privacy/reveal assessment and explicit authorization.
4. If a private truth matters to a feature, write a neutral blocked task; ask an authorized story authority for the precise contract via a **private** channel.
5. Material derived from a proposal must remain tagged PROPOSAL. Repo-inclusion is not canon promotion.
