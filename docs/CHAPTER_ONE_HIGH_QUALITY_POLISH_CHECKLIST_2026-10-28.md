### The player promise

Within the *first playable chapter*, the player should understand **why Ender needs work**, why **Ionia** trusts his unusual harmonic-engine knowledge, why the first flight fails despite tangible preparation, and why surviving together matters. The ocean splashdown should feel consequential but controlled; the later family meeting should earn Lyra's involvement, not instantaneously teleport her aboard. The player must physically gather and install materials, see the failed retest, and only then discover that Ionia's anxious humming of Denith's old shanty encodes a resonance sequence Ender can interpret. The chapter's emotional payoff is collaborative understanding—not free fuel, a premature relaunch or automated combat magic.

### Checklist protocol

Each item has a stable ID, priority (**P0 = blocks playable chapter**, **P1 = blocks high-quality visual/audio/art acceptance**, **P2 = release/hardware-scale follow-up**), named discipline and measurable acceptance. **Evidence required** always means a URL to exact-head run or file artifact **plus** a reviewer note where visual/emotional quality is involved. Never mark `[x]` from a previous head, placeholder screenshot, speculative prose or a promise. **Open** is deliberately not the same as **failed**.

To close an item, add a dated acceptance receipt in the final evidence table: source SHA, test/run ID, image/audio/build SHA-256, reviewer, disposition and remaining caveats. A code/CI green badge cannot independently close art, narrative-emotion, human playability, security or shipping gates. Fail-closed when source, licensing or ownership is unknown.

- [ ] **CH1-16** [P1 | Art] **Consistent character identity.** Acceptance: Ender and Ionia retain approved visual silhouette, correct scale and contact across four viewing directions and dialogue; Lyra and Talen have independently reviewed art/animation, no placeholder reference promoted. - [ ] **CH1-17** [P1 | Cinematography] **Shot continuity from dock to ship to sea.** Acceptance: actual Aureate Wake shape, bridge/geodesic dome, accessible forward deck, engineering geography, waterline, first-flight warning and splashdown are visually consistent; camera framing never hides the active subject. Evidence required: fixed + moving matched camera captures, source model/scene identifiers.
- [ ] **CH1-18** [P1 | Environment] **Atmosphere and texture quality.** Acceptance: dock timber/shoreline, forest/biome ground, sky, fog and waves use coherent physical datum; no flat disconnected slabs, repetitive shader bands, floating foliage, tile seams or unapproved atlas. Evidence required: true native day/night/weather/screenshots and art director comparison.
- [ ] **CH1-19** [P1 | VFX] **Cause-and-effect readable in motion.** Acceptance: engine phase warnings, protective shielding, ocean contact, wet hull and stable/unstable harmonics change at the **correct state event**, with low-motion and reduced-quality fallbacks; no VFX grants game-state truth. Evidence required: 30-second native sequence, event-to-frame receipts and safety review.
- [ ] **CH1-20** [P1 | Interface] **Readable high-density dialogue and HUD.** Acceptance: no text clipping or overlays at 960×640 and 1280×720, keyboard focus and speaker identities are clear; text reveal/advance never skips unshown mandatory lines; fonts remain legible at tested OS scaling. Evidence required: full-route screenshots, bounds assertions, player eye-tracking/notes.
- [ ] **CH1-21** [P1 | Audio] **Owned music, dialogue, and restrained mix.** Acceptance: actual legally usable soundtrack source is manifest-bound and auditioned, cue changes/crossfades are scene-timed, effects/music/dialogue can be adjusted separately, and shanty is an original approved composition rather than a sine-wave test or borrowed song. Evidence required: provenance/hash/license, audible recording on target hardware, mix review.
- [ ] **CH1-22** [P1 | Accessibility] **Equivalent information for all senses.** Acceptance: every engine phase and melody clue also appears in readable text/visuals, captions identify speakers and non-speech sound, reduced flashing/motion is respected, color is never the sole signal and controls work keyboard-only and gamepad-only. Evidence required: accessibility walkthrough, input/mute/high-contrast QA.
- [ ] **CH1-23** [P1 | Emotion] **Relationship scenes have breathing room.** Acceptance: preflight anticipation, ocean aftermath, Talen's worry and Ionia's anxious humming have appropriate pauses and acting beats; no mandatory unskippable stall, mystery spoiled in advance or repetitive exposition. Evidence required: creator narrative review and blind player interviews.
- [ ] **CH1-24** [P1 | Gameplay] **Interaction feel and state feedback.** Acceptance: movement acceleration, target range, prompts, bounce/retry, camera response and controls are consistent between dock, engineering, waterborne and family/bench; invalid inputs explain why no progress occurred. Evidence required: input trace, 60-second gameplay capture and tester feedback.
- [ ] **CH1-25** [P1 | QA] **Real gameplay screenshots and video, not only art mockups.** Acceptance: exact-head manifest indexes at least flyer, hiring, preflight, first flight, contact/float, reserve, berth, family, theory, pickup, failed retest, shanty, interval chart and bench; provenance, camera, resolution, SHA-256 and original/approved flags accompany each. Evidence required: native artifact packet + independently inspected video.
- [ ] **CH1-26** [P1 | Playtest] **Uncoached human first-hour comprehension.** Acceptance: ≥3 independent fresh players can start the installed candidate, explain their objective, reach the agreed preview end state and recover from one mistake without developer instruction; log confusion/time/defects individually. This is a proposed gate, **not** a study already performed. Evidence required: dated anonymized raw playtest receipts, task completion times and facilitator notes.

### Gate C · Performance, install and release readiness — P2

- [ ] **CH1-27** [P2 | Performance] **Representative Windows graphics budget.** Acceptance: agree target hardware first; record cold shader compilation, 1% lows, average FPS, input latency and memory during the dock/ship/ocean/shanty transitions at high and reduced detail. Proposed acceptance target: **60 FPS typical / ≥30 FPS reduced** on expressly approved hardware, subject to review. Evidence required: device specs, native capture and frame-time traces, not hosted software GL only.
- [ ] **CH1-28** [P2 | Stability] **Repeatable full chapter, no leak/softlock.** Acceptance: three clean restarts and full route completions, repeated save/load, scene transition/backtracking, idle pause, minimized/restored window and failure recovery without crashes or memory growth beyond agreed budget. Evidence required: reproducible test protocol, native logs and independent player run.
- [ ] **CH1-29** [P2 | Packaging] **Installer and local standalone window.** Acceptance: downloadable, signed/clearly risk-reviewed Windows setup performs clean per-user install, creates shortcut, launches a resizable/maximizable Godot game, preserves saves on uninstall and refuses unsafe overwrites. Evidence required: exact source/export/setup hashes and verified real Windows install/relaunch/uninstall. **Do not** publish a link to an unpublished artifact.
- [ ] **CH1-30** [P2 | Security] **Unchanged Defender-on custody.** Acceptance: real-time Windows Defender enabled throughout an exact-build scan and packaging audit, with artifact provenance and no AV bypass; functional install proof never substitutes for security clearance. Evidence required: custody logs, runner details and signoff.
- [ ] **CH1-31** [P2 | Rights] **Assets and provenance cleared.** Acceptance: art, shaders, references, font licenses, audio, original shanty and any archival songs have explicit rightsholder/source, usage scope and checksums before public inclusion; no developer-only preview file leaks. Evidence required: resolved license records, source hashes, redistribution scopes and asset manifest.
- [ ] **CH1-32** [P2 | Release] **Creator-approved publishable Chapter One preview.** Acceptance: exact final source+installer SHA, all P0 closed, creator-approved relevant P1/P2 waivers, no P0 defects, named known limitations, a noncanonical/restricted-story spoiler audit, documented fallback/rollback and explicit release authorization. Evidence required: signed human review and actual verified GitHub Release link after publication.
